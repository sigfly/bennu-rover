# Fossil reconstruction references

The hunt is a fictional game feature; no dinosaur remains are present on Bennu. The models are original procedural reconstructions, not museum scans or copied photos. Visual references: [historic Smithsonian quarry photo](https://www.si.edu/object/dinosaur-skeleton-exposed-dinosaur-quarry-utah%3Asiris_arc_367763), [USGS quarry photo](https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/dinosaur-national-monument-dinosaur-quarry-1), [NPS exposed bonebed](https://www.nps.gov/dino/planyourvisit/quarry-exhibit-hall.htm), and [recent excavation](https://www.nps.gov/dino/learn/news/parkinglotfossilsexcavated.htm). The excavated pose imitates articulated and partly disarticulated remains embedded in matrix, not upright exhibition mounts.
