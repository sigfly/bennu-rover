The files part-00.bin to part-25.bin are consecutive raw sections of the unchanged 1,277,496-byte GLB. SHA-256: 0d8f272006245a54656bea5de7b44b0f535ec6d761f64a0044d8d88f2d2c9380. tools/build.py reconstructs the binary and checks its hash and triangle count.

The source is procedural Blender geometry (AI-assisted generator code, not AI-generated geometry or textures). In the app the model is rescaled, converted to monochrome and given separately articulated suspension components.
